# PRODUCT VISION — Verce 3D | Laboratório de Custos

## 1. The problem

A small 3D-printing shop makes pricing decisions with incomplete information. Cost is spread
across filament spools bought at different prices, small consumables (rings, screws, magnets,
glue, varnish), packaging, machine wear, electricity and time. Marketplaces then take a
commission that silently destroys the margin the operator thought they had.

The typical result is a spreadsheet that:

- mixes estimated and real cost until neither is trustworthy;
- loses the reason a past price was what it was;
- is recalculated retroactively when a filament price changes, corrupting history;
- cannot answer "which product actually makes money on Shopee?".

## 2. The product

Verce 3D is the operational system for that shop. It turns cost into an explicit,
auditable, reproducible calculation and makes the margin visible **before** the sale.

Three ideas define the product:

1. **The Laboratory.** A place to simulate the cost of an idea — filaments, supplies, time,
   energy — without creating a customer, a quote or a sale. Simulation is the entry point to
   the domain, not an afterthought.
2. **Frozen history.** A quote is a statement made on a date. Nothing that happens afterwards
   to prices, fees or recipes may change what that statement said. Revisions are added, never
   overwritten.
3. **Estimated versus actual.** The plan said 90 g; the printer used 96 g. The system stores
   both and derives the variance, because that gap is where the real margin lives.

## 3. Who uses it

| Persona | Need |
|---|---|
| **Owner/operator** (primary, daily) | Quote fast, price correctly, know what is profitable |
| **Production assistant** (future) | See the queue, print the shop-floor order, mark actual consumption |
| **Accountant/advisor** (occasional) | Export sales, expenses, margins for a period |

Initially a single organization, a handful of users. Not a SaaS.

## 4. Product principles

1. **Explainability beats sophistication.** Every number must be able to show its own
   arithmetic. A price the operator cannot explain to a customer is a defect.
2. **Determinism.** Same inputs, same engine version, same output — always.
3. **The past is read-only.** Prices, fees and recipes evolve; documents already issued do not.
4. **Configuration, not code.** Marketplace rules, validity periods, label sizes, tax and
   rounding policies are data an operator can change.
5. **Few clicks for frequent actions.** New quote, new customer, new experiment are one click
   from the home screen.
6. **Desktop-first, responsive, accessible.** The operator works at a desk; the phone is for
   checking, not for data entry.
7. **Grow by extension.** New material types, new channels, new document types and new report
   dimensions must be additions, not rewrites.

## 5. What the product is not (v1)

- Not a fiscal/NF-e system. Documents are commercial, not tax documents.
- Not a multi-tenant SaaS. One organization.
- Not an accounting ledger. Expenses are managerial, not double-entry bookkeeping.
- Not an MES. Production is a queue with statuses, not machine orchestration.
- Not a storefront. No public catalog, no checkout.

## 6. Success criteria

The product is successful when the operator can answer, without a spreadsheet:

- What does this product cost me, itemized, right now?
- What should I charge on Shopee to keep a 35% margin?
- Why was quote `260906-4B` priced at R$ 128,40 on that date?
- Which product had the largest gap between estimated and actual filament use last month?
- Which channel produced the most profit — not the most revenue — this quarter?
- How much filament and energy did I consume in September?

## 7. Domain glossary (product-level)

| Term (pt-BR) | Term (code) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Filamento | Filament | Printing material, priced per kilogram |
| Insumo | Supply | Any non-filament consumable or component |
| Embalagem | Packaging | A supply used to pack the finished item |
| Receita / Ficha técnica | ProductRecipe | The BOM plus process parameters of a product |
| Laboratório | CostExperiment | A cost simulation with no commercial effect |
| Orçamento | Quote | A priced commercial proposal to a customer |
| Revisão | QuoteRevision | An immutable version of a quote |
| Canal de venda | SalesChannel | Direct sale or a marketplace |
| Taxa | FeeRule | The commission/fixed fee a channel charges |
| Pedido de produção | ProductionOrder | Shop-floor work generated by an approved quote |
| Custo previsto | Estimated cost | Cost computed at quote/simulation time |
| Custo real | Actual cost | Cost measured after production |

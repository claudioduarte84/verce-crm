# S8C.0 — Brazil marketplace capability matrix

Research date: **2026-09-24**. Decisions below are proposed **structural** provider support for the eight frozen S8B codes. They do not change the `UNKNOWN` seeds, confer an account grant, or imply runtime availability. `SUPPORTED` is bounded by the evidence summary in its row; it does not claim every subfeature in the mission checklist. `UNKNOWN` rows name the missing official evidence and remain disabled in future implementation until resolved. Confidence describes the *decision's evidence*, not operational uptime. Source IDs and full titles are in [the official evidence appendix](S8C0-OFFICIAL-PROVIDER-DISCOVERY.md#evidence-appendix).

## Consequences for capability state

Do **not** migrate or edit seeded `UNKNOWN` values from this document. In a later, separately reviewed implementation, an official structural decision may be applied to `MarketplaceProviderCapability`; a seller's `MarketplaceAccountCapability` remains `UNKNOWN` until its actual grant is established. `SUPPORTED + GRANTED + active` can make a capability effective; a 429, expired token, missing scope, or outage is runtime state and must not mutate structural support. Shopee and the unresolved TikTok cells require another official documentation/entitlement gate before use. No row is `UNSUPPORTED` because absence was not demonstrated by an authoritative negative statement.
